Expedition Overview & Trail Summary

The ultimate high-altitude mountain journey on Earth. Traverse the mighty 63km Baltoro Glacier through Paiju, Urdukas, and Goro II to arrive at Concordia—the legendary "Throne Room of the Mountain Gods"—surrounded by four 8,000m giants: K2 (8,611m), Broad Peak (8,051m), Gasherbrum I (8,080m), and Gasherbrum II (8,035m). Cap the expedition with a technical fixed-rope ascent over the glaciated Gondogoro La (5,585m) down into the lush green Hushe Valley.

Key Route Highlights

360° panoramic amphitheater of four 8,000m peaks at Concordia
Direct foot trek to K2 Base Camp (5,150m) and Art Gilkey Memorial
Spectacular crossing of glaciated Gondogoro La Pass (5,585m)
Views of Trango Towers, Cathedral Peak, Masherbrum (7,821m) & Laila Peak
Certified high-altitude Balti guides, safety ropes, and full camp kitchen

Expedition Visual Gallery

Day 1

Arrival in Islamabad

Elev: 540m0 hrs

Meet our expedition team at Islamabad International Airport. Transfer to 4-star hotel. Briefing at Ministry of Tourism & Alpine Club.

Day 2

Scenic Flight to Skardu

Elev: 2,228m0 hrs

Spectacular mountain flight past Nanga Parbat to Skardu. (Contingency: Overland drive via Karakoram Highway in case of flight cancellation).

Day 3

Acclimatization & Skardu Bazar

Elev: 2,228m2 hrs

Visit historic Kharpocho Fort, organic village, and check high-altitude mountaineering permits with tour leader.

Day 4

4x4 Jeep Drive to Askole

Elev: 3,000m0 hrs (Jeep 6-7 hrs)

Thrilling off-road drive through Shigar Valley, traversing rugged gorges along the Braldu River to Askole—the last inhabited village.

Day 5

Askole to Jhola Camp

Elev: 3,100m6-7 hrs

First day of trekking. Walk along the Braldu river bank, crossing the suspension bridge at the confluence of the Dumordo river.

Day 6

Jhola to Paiju Camp

Elev: 3,450m6-7 hrs

Trek through sand tracks and willow trees to Paiju camp beneath the sheer granite spires of Paiju Peak (6,610m).

Day 7

Rest & Acclimatization Day at Paiju

Elev: 3,450m0 hrs

Crucial acclimatization day. Porters bake traditional bread and perform Balti songs and dances.

Day 8

Paiju to Khoburtse (Baltoro Snout)

Elev: 3,800m5-6 hrs

Step onto the snout of the legendary 63km long Baltoro Glacier. Views of Trango Towers and Cathedral Spire unfold.

Day 9

Khoburtse to Urdukas

Elev: 4,050m4-5 hrs

Trek along lateral moraine to grassy cliffs of Urdukas with jaw-dropping views across the glacier to Great Trango.

Day 10

Urdukas to Goro II

Elev: 4,300m6-7 hrs

Trek in the center of Baltoro Glacier surrounded by Masherbrum (7,821m), Muztagh Tower, and Biarchedi.

Day 11

Goro II to Concordia

Elev: 4,600m5-6 hrs

Arrive at Concordia—the pinnacle junction of Baltoro & Godwin-Austen glaciers. Front-row vistas of K2 and Broad Peak.

Day 12

Concordia to K2 Base Camp Excursion

Elev: 5,150m7-8 hrs

Day trek to Broad Peak Base Camp, Gilkey Memorial, and K2 Base Camp (5,150m). Return to Concordia camp.

Day 13

Concordia to Ali Camp

Elev: 4,800m5-6 hrs

Trek across Vigne Glacier toward the base of Gondogoro La. Early dinner and pre-pass safety briefing.

Day 14

Cross Gondogoro La (5,585m) to Khuspang

Elev: 5,585m9-11 hrs

Midnight alpine start with crampons and fixed ropes over Gondogoro La. Sunrise panorama of 4 eight-thousanders and Laila Peak. Descend to Khuspang.

Day 15

Khuspang to Saicho

Elev: 3,350m5-6 hrs

Follow the Gondogoro glacier down to the green pastures and meltwater streams of Saicho.

Day 16

Saicho to Hushe Village & Drive to Skardu

Elev: 2,228m3 hrs walk + 4 hrs drive

Short walk to Hushe village, meet 4x4 Jeeps and drive back through Khaplu to Skardu hotel for hot showers.

Day 17

Fly Skardu to Islamabad

Elev: 540m0 hrs

Morning flight to capital city Islamabad. Farewell dinner with traditional cuisine.

Day 18

Contingency & Islamabad Sightseeing

Elev: 540m0 hrs

Buffer day for mountain flight delays. Visit Faisal Mosque, Pakistan Monument, and Lok Virsa.

Day 19

International Departure

Elev: 540m0 hrs

Airport transfer for your onward flight home with lifelong memories of Karakoram.
